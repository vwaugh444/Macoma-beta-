Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-21

Draft. Check this against your state's privacy laws (for example CCPA/CPRA) and list your actual subprocessors before launch.

What we collect

  • Your number and consent record: when and how you agreed to receive texts, and the version of the terms you agreed to.
  • Your messages and Macoma's replies, including recent conversation history.
  • What you ask Macoma to keep: notes, contacts (names, numbers, emails), reminders, invoices and your profile (name, business, time zone, payment link).
  • An action log of what Macoma did or proposed for you.
  • Basic technical data: your IP address when you sign up on the website, and server logs.

How we use it

We use this information to run the assistant for you, prevent abuse, and fix problems. We don't use your content to train AI models, and we don't sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • AI model provider: receives your messages so it can write the replies.
  • SMS provider (Twilio): delivers texts.
  • Stripe: receives the details needed for invoices you choose to send.
  • Other AI agents or paid web services: receive only what's needed for a task you asked for.

Your contacts' details are only used to message them when you approve it.

Your choices

Text DELETE MY DATA to delete your account. Deleting it also cancels your plan and disconnects any accounts you linked. We keep only your consent record, which carrier rules and the law require, and a one-way fingerprint (hash) of your number so the same number can't start a second free trial. Text STOP to stop texts. To access or export your data, email [support email].

Security and retention

We use access controls and encryption in transit. Conversation history is limited to recent messages. Deleted accounts are removed from active systems right away and from backups within [N] days.
