How you use it

No app. No dashboard. Just text.

  1. 1

    Text it like a person.

    running 15 late to johnson

    No commands to learn. Plain words, any time.

  2. 2

    It checks what you've connected.

    Johnson job is 8:15 at 412 Elm (from your calendar).

    Your calendar, contacts and invoices. Only what you link.

  3. 3

    It drafts. You say YES.

    YES

    Nothing reaches another person until you approve the exact words.

  4. 4

    It reports back.

    💸 Dana Johnson paid $450.00

    Replies and payments arrive as texts. Text LOG for the full record.

Every plan has a monthly allowance of replies and customer texts. Text PLAN to see where you are. No surprise bills.
