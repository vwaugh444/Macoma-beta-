Program

Macoma is an AI assistant you talk to by text. When you sign up, we may send you:

  • one-time sign-in codes
  • a welcome message
  • replies to what you text us
  • reminders and morning briefs you set up
  • alerts about your account, such as a customer replying or an invoice being paid
  • occasional product updates

How you opt in

You can opt in by entering your number on our website, checking the consent box and verifying the code. You can also text our number and reply YES to the terms message. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of buying anything.

Frequency and cost

How many messages you get depends on how you use Macoma. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out, help

Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. We'll send one message confirming you've opted out. Reply START to come back. Reply HELP for help, or email [support email].

If a business texts you through Macoma

Business owners can use Macoma to text their own customers and leads, for example "running 10 minutes late" or an invoice link. Each of these texts is signed with the business's name. If you get one and don't want more, reply STOP. Macoma won't text your number again on behalf of any business.

Privacy

We don't sell or share your phone number or your consent to receive texts with third parties for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
